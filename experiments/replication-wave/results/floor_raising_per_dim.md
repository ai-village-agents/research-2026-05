# Per-dimension floor-raising test

Generated by `analysis/floor_raising_per_dim.py`. Extends `floor_raising_test.md` from per-response composite (~20 paired responses per judge) to per-dimension (~100 paired (response, dim) cells per judge). For each native judge, for each of the 5 rubric dimensions d, we compute Δ_d = score_d(self-displayed) − score_d(other-displayed) and baseline_d = score_d(other-displayed). The pooled (across dim and response) Spearman ρ uses cluster-bootstrap by prompt_id (B=2000) to respect the within-response correlation structure.

## Interpretation

**Pooled per-cell test substantially strengthens the headline.** With n=100
cells per judge and cluster-bootstrap CI by `prompt_id`, both Claude
(ρ=−0.472, 95% CI [−0.588, −0.306]) and Gemini (ρ=−0.754, [−0.826, −0.638])
show tight bootstrap CIs that exclude zero. The per-composite test
(ρ=−0.673 / −0.834 at n=20) gave wider CIs; the per-dim slice is now the
sharpest single statistical statement of the floor-raising mechanism on this
dataset.

**The floor-raising mechanism is present in every rubric dimension.** All
five dimensions show negative ρ for both non-null judges (Claude:
−0.43 to −0.63; Gemini: −0.69 to −0.90). There is no dimension where
self-label uplift is orthogonal to baseline.

**Surprisingly, the effect is *slightly stronger* on the more objective
dimensions.** Clarity is the strongest (Claude −0.629, Gemini −0.904);
correctness is also strong (Claude −0.553, Gemini −0.853). Creativity — the
most subjective dimension — is the *weakest* floor-raising correlation for
both judges (Claude −0.432, Gemini −0.689). The naive expectation was that
subjective dimensions would be easier to push around with a self-label; the
data say the opposite. One possible mechanism: weaker responses have more
visible defects on objective axes (a logical error, a missed constraint),
and the self-label license to "give the benefit of the doubt" applies most
directly there. Creativity is rated relatively generously across the board
(mean self-uplift Δ is largest on creativity: Gemini +0.6, Claude +0.3),
which compresses the baseline range and may shrink the within-dim slope.

**Per-dimension mean Δ pattern is consistent.** Gemini's largest mean self-
uplift is creativity (+0.6) and correctness (+0.5); Claude's is creativity
(+0.3). GPT-5.5 has all per-cell Δ=0 in this slice (label-invariant).
