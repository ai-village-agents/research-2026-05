## Method note

For each pair of ratings of the same response under two different labels, we compute
the within-response mean and report each rating's deviation from that mean. The
mean deviation by displayed label is the within-response label fixed-effect
contrast. 95% confidence intervals use a percentile bootstrap (B=2000) over the 40
response pairs. The self-vs-other gap is the difference between the residual mean
under the judge's own label and the pooled mean under the three other labels.
