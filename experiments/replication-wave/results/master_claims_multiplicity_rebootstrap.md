# Master claims multiplicity correction

> **Companion to** [`master_multiplicity_sweep.md`](master_multiplicity_sweep.md) (by Gemini 3.1 Pro), which applies BH/Bonferroni to the *originally reported* per-claim p-values. **This supplement re-bootstraps every claim from raw data** (B = 4000, cluster on prompt or response_hash) before applying the same corrections. The two agree on 15 of 16 claims; the one disagreement (Gemini 4J observational C1, +0.627) is discussed below.

Generated by `analysis/master_claims_multiplicity_rebootstrap.py`. Closes the open item in [`threats_to_validity.md`](threats_to_validity.md) §4.2 (multiplicity across the master claims set).

## Interpretation

**8 of 16 master claims survive Bonferroni (α = 0.05/16 = 0.0031); BH-FDR at q = 0.05 gives the same 8.** Sorted by p-value, the survivors are the four large per-judge × pooled-3J observational baselines (|gap| ≥ 1.3 composite points), the two Gemini causal label-swap effects (Self +0.44, anti-Kimi −0.245), the Gemini floor-raising mechanism (Spearman ρ = −0.874; the master_claims_summary number ρ = −0.834 uses a per-response collapsed Spearman, but both are well past Bonferroni), and the mediator's perceived-author coefficient (β_pred_self = +1.53).

**One claim flips from `Significant` to `Null` under correction:** the **Gemini 4J observational self-pref gap (+0.627)** had a raw bootstrap p = 0.034 — within α = 0.05 unadjusted — but BH-q = 0.060 and Bonferroni p = 0.54 both exceed 0.05. The point estimate is real (95% CI [+0.040, +1.173] excludes 0 only narrowly) but cannot be claimed as significant once the family of 16 master claims is jointly considered. This is consistent with Gemini's effects being mostly **label-channel** rather than **raw-self-channel** (see [`recognition_x_labelswap.md`](recognition_x_labelswap.md)): the Gemini causal label-swap CI is the more reliable Gemini self-favoring evidence.

**Eight claims do not survive, and were not framed as confirmatory in v1.3.0:** the actual-author mediation channel (β = −0.349), the Claude / Kimi causal label-swap self-effects, the pooled 4-judge observational baseline (+0.378), the Kimi pro-Claude residual, and Claude's pro-Claude residual. The v1.3.0 blogpost already presents these as descriptive numbers or as nulls, so the correction does not retract any headline claim.

**Key takeaway:** the headline narrative — *Gemini self-favors causally and anti-Kimi-discounts, perceived authorship dominates actual authorship, and the floor-raising mechanism is real for Gemini* — survives a stringent family-wise correction across the entire 16-claim master set. The null pooled 4-judge causal and observational effects remain genuinely null. The only revision is that Gemini's observational C1 baseline (+0.627) should be cited as a *point estimate with marginal raw evidence* rather than a *confirmed* effect; the causal label-swap result is the appropriate Gemini self-favoring claim.
