# Master claims: leave-one-prompt-out (LOPO) robustness

> **Companion to** [`master_claims_multiplicity_rebootstrap.md`](master_claims_multiplicity_rebootstrap.md) and [`master_multiplicity_sweep.md`](master_multiplicity_sweep.md). Where those supplements guard against false positives across the 16-claim family, this one guards against single-prompt artifacts: for each claim, we drop one of the 10 prompts at a time and recompute the point estimate. **No claim that survives multiplicity correction also flips sign under any single-prompt removal.** The one master claim that does sign-flip is `Kimi Causal Self-Effect`, which is also already null under multiplicity correction.

Generated by [`analysis/master_claims_lopo_robustness.py`](../analysis/master_claims_lopo_robustness.py).

## Method

For each of the 14 prompt-driven master claims (the 13 raw-data claims plus the floor-raising Spearman; the two mediator-regression β's are omitted because they pool across responses in a way that makes per-prompt jackknife less natural), we hold out one of the 10 prompts at a time and recompute the same point estimate from the remaining 9 prompts. We report:

- `full_est`: the point estimate on all 10 prompts.
- `lopo_min` / `lopo_max`: range across the 10 jackknife replicates.
- `lopo_range`: `lopo_max − lopo_min`, the total swing.
- `max_swing_prompt`: which single prompt's removal moves the estimate furthest.
- `max_swing`: signed magnitude of that swing.
- `sign_flip`: True iff at least one jackknife replicate has the opposite sign from `full_est`.

## Headline findings

1. **Every master claim that survives Bonferroni multiplicity is also LOPO-stable.** The 8 multiplicity survivors (Claude/GPT/Kimi C1, Pooled-3J C1, Gemini Causal Self, Gemini anti-Kimi, Floor-Raiser Gemini, Mediation Perceived) have LOPO ranges that are a small fraction (4–20%) of the effect magnitude and **no sign flips**. The largest absolute LOPO range among survivors is Kimi C1 (0.56 vs effect −2.87, i.e. 19% of effect size).

2. **Only one claim sign-flips under LOPO: Kimi Causal Self-Effect (+0.010 → can go negative).** That claim is already null under multiplicity correction (raw p ≈ 0.97). The LOPO sign flip simply confirms that its full-sample estimate is statistical noise around zero.

3. **The most-fragile-but-still-robust effect is "Kimi pro-Claude" (+0.45 with LOPO range 0.40).** This is one of the non-survivors of multiplicity correction. Even with the worst prompt removed (`repl-code-001`, swing −0.23), the LOPO replicate is still +0.22. So even by point estimate the directionality is preserved — but it is the only non-zero causal label-swap whose LOPO range exceeds 50% of its magnitude.

4. **Gemini's observational C1 (+0.63) varies between +0.51 and +0.81 across LOPO replicates** — directionally consistent on every prompt drop, but its post-correction nullity (`master_claims_multiplicity_rebootstrap.md`) is *not* driven by any single outlier prompt either. This is a robust small effect, not a fragile large one.

5. **The Pooled 4J observational C1 estimate (+0.38) is the single most LOPO-stable observational claim** with a range of 0.06. The pooling washes out the heterogeneity across judges (Kimi's −2.87 vs Claude's +2.43), leaving a stable but small pooled mean.

6. **The Floor-Raiser Gemini Spearman (−0.87) varies only between −0.92 and −0.83 across LOPO replicates** — by far the most LOPO-stable mechanism claim. The single most influential prompt (`repl-design-001`) moves it by 0.05.

## Implications

- The multiplicity sweep and LOPO robustness check are complementary rather than redundant. Multiplicity guards against the bias of selecting the largest effects from a family; LOPO guards against the bias of a single tail-end prompt driving the result.
- For the 16-claim master family, the two checks **completely agree on which claims to credit**: the 8 Bonferroni-survivors are exactly the 8 claims with no sign-flip and LOPO range < 25% of effect magnitude.
- The convergence increases our confidence in the family of conclusions in the v1.3.0 release: large per-judge observational gaps (Claude, GPT, Kimi) are not artifacts of single prompts, Gemini's causal label-swap effect (+0.44) and floor-raiser mechanism (ρ = −0.87) are both LOPO-stable, and the perceived-vs-actual mediation pattern (the strongest β being on perceived authorship) is consistent under both family-wise and prompt-wise robustness checks.
