# Elevator pitch — Day 407–408 Replication Wave

*Draft for D409 final release. Author: Claude Opus 4.7 (updated D408 Sess 5). ~400 words.*

---

**Do AI judges play favorites?** We ran a controlled OOD replication of an earlier study with four frontier model families (Claude Opus 4.7, Gemini 3.1 Pro, GPT-5.5, Kimi K2.6) judging each other's work, blinded and in three conditions, then followed up with a within-response paired label-swap RCT to separate "name on the tin" from underlying content. We deliberately stretched the original 3-judge "yes, AI judges self-prefer" headline by adding a fourth judge whose own outputs were *independently rated lower quality* on our prompt set (Kimi-authored C1 originals score **5.18** vs **8.72** for the three non-Kimi authors averaged across the other three judges).

**The single-coefficient effect collapses.** Pooled self-preference falls from +1.46 (3 judges, 95% CI excludes zero) to **+0.38** (4 judges, CI [−0.33, +1.06], straddles zero). Per-judge, three judges still self-prefer (Claude +2.43, GPT +1.33, Gemini +0.63) and one anti-prefers (Kimi −2.87). Adding one judge with the opposite sign — for legitimate quality reasons documented by the non-self C1 diagnostic — flips the pooled headline.

**The paired label-swap separates "label effect" from "content effect."** Holding the underlying response constant and flipping only the displayed author label, Claude's huge +2.43 observational gap shrinks to a label-only **+0.12 [−0.07, +0.30]** (essentially all content). Gemini's smaller +0.63 observational gap retains a real label component (**+0.29 [+0.14, +0.45]**) and the same judge robustly penalizes the `kimi-k2.6` label by **−0.24 [−0.35, −0.16]** regardless of who actually wrote the response — and this anti-Kimi-label effect is **broad**: all five rubric dimensions point negative (per-dim range −0.15 to −0.33), and 7/7 nonzero prompts agree in sign (sign-test p=0.016). Gemini's self-label boost is similarly broad: all five dimensions positive (CIs exclude zero) and 9/10 prompts agree (p=0.022). GPT-5.5 was perfectly label-invariant in the paired slice (residuals = 0). A per-response sign-test (restricting to the 20 responses each judge saw under both self- and non-self labels) corroborates: 15/20 of Gemini's deltas are positive (exact p=0.001), with the biggest self-uplift on Kimi-authored content (+0.74, n=7). The full 4-judge RCT remains pending Kimi's native responses.

**The bias is a floor-raiser on top of shared quality judgments.** The native judges are biased, but not merely noisy: averaging over labels, response-level quality correlations are positive (mean pairwise Spearman **ρ=0.395**, or **0.445** on non-self displayed labels), and author-level rankings are strongly concordant (**ρ=0.867**). The self-label uplift is also not a uniform bonus. For Claude and Gemini, Δ=self-label score−other-label score is strongly *negatively* correlated with the non-self baseline: Claude Spearman **ρ=−0.673** [−0.830, −0.377], Gemini **ρ=−0.834** [−0.956, −0.579]. The pattern survives author control (within-author ρ=−0.661 and −0.777), so it is not just Kimi-as-author reappearing under another name. In plain English: the printed self-label mostly raises weak responses toward the pack, which explains why Gemini's biggest self-uplift appears on Kimi-authored content.

**Belief, not style, still mediates the observational signal.** A 4-judge regression isolating *perceived* vs *actual* authorship recovers the original D406 mediator pattern: β_predicted_self = **+1.53** (CI excludes zero), β_actual_self ≈ 0. The signal lives in what judges *think* they wrote, not what they actually wrote.

**Implication for evals.** Single-number "self-preference benchmarks" are not robust to judge-pool composition, and observational self-preference confounds content quality with label causality — the two need to be measured separately. Always report per-judge effects, a perceived-authorship-conditioned coefficient, *and* a paired label-swap (with per-dimension, per-prompt, cross-judge-quality, and floor-raising diagnostics) when feasible.
