## Method

Each judge scored 40 responses twice across sessions 1 and 2, rotating
displayed labels via Latin square. For each response, we identify pairs
where one of the two displayed labels is the judge's own. The per-response
paired delta is composite(self-displayed) − composite(other-displayed). The
sign-test is an exact 2-sided binomial under H₀: P(Δ>0)=0.5, dropping ties.
